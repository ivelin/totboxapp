## Why (plain English)

The host LLM got fat. Grok Bot + Booking Agent already finds vendors, fills website forms, and reads mail. Totbox was a thin PM sidecar next to a thin EA. That split died.

Bootstrap OS v2.8: evidence beats narrative; do not protect time spent. Totbox was coded into journey 6 while Phase 4 real proof never closed. Ready for human eyes is still **unknown**. There are **zero** redacted stage-6 household job notes.

Phase 1 cleaning locked 2026-07-30. About 17 days later, the last product-adjacent work (2026-08-08) was public-data research, not a house job.

The homepage still sells a dual-sided story — “families book activities, care, and home services.” That is **assumed capability** / an old story, not a closed proof.

Own ICP named the kill: free chat AI is good enough. The old comparator “Gmail alone” is obsolete. Status quo is **Grok Bot + Booking Agent + calendar**.
