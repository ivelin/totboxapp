## Who

Metro-level composite: owner-occupant or head of household who alone owns money, access, and booking rights for recurring HVAC preventive/membership and house cleaning—no second household approver. Same services and human channels as dual-income; different approval topology. Public-safe pattern only; not a named person.

## Job to be done

Keep recurring HVAC and cleaning jobs moving end-to-end on one person’s clock: structured brief → gated outreach → quote normalize → self-approve money/time → confirm → complete → next-due, with fewer human touchpoints and no partner second-approve stall.
