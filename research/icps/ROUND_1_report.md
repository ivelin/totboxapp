## Primary recommendation + kill criteria

### Primary recommendation

**Treat dual-income recurring (HVAC + cleaning chore PM) as Shape A real-test priority only.** Keep all `promote_lean=hold`. Do **not** promote primary focus, claim PMF, or open multi-ICP real GTM until dual-income shows instrumented touchpoint drop and non-zero pay-or-act.

**Sequence:**

1. Founder gate this pack (`iterate` / `agree_ready` / `kill`).  
2. If `agree_ready` for real tests: **only** dual-income first.  
3. Defer remote / tree / SMS / operator real pipelines until dual-income evidence or explicit beachhead stall.  
4. Operator LOI samples only **after** household jobs produce operator-acceptable structured briefs.  
5. Re-rank after real sample — do not lock READY_FOR_REAL_WORLD on synthetic alone.

### Dual-income kill / demote criteria (active)

Kill or demote this ICP if:

1. Honest shadow HVAC/cleaning jobs **fail to cut documented touchpoints** vs status-quo email/calendar chaos after a fair try.  
2. Evidence shows a **vendor directory/marketplace is required** for value.  
3. Households refuse human approval gates / dry-run **or** demand full unsupervised send.  
4. Partner dual-approval **permanently blocks** completion (unfinished-job rate kills learning).  
5. After synthetic + real filter, **pay-or-act stays near zero** (no repeat use, referral, soft price, or waitlist friction).  
6. Safety/channel incidents (wrong send, access/scope mishap) make solo-operated household jobs untenable without heavy ops.

### Thesis-level kill

- No meaningful touchpoint drop on real jobs, **or**  
- Directory/marketplace proves required for value.
