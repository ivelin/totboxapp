# ICP: household-dual-income-recurring

**Round 1-r2 recommended rank:** 2 (topology A/B + multi-party pain peer)

## Who

Time-poor dual-income or dual-career households that already know (or can find) providers and must coordinate recurring preventive HVAC and house cleaning—plus partner approvals—mostly over email/PDF and calendar windows. Metro-level composite; not a named person.

## Job to be done

When a recurring home service is due or quotes arrive, collapse research→brief→compare→approve→book→complete→next-due into a visible job with human gates, fewer touchpoints, and rebook without re-researching last year’s provider.

## Pain

Multi-turn quote/scope loops (memberships, inclusions, cancel fees), partner forward-and-approve latency, calendar negotiation, forgotten next-due preventive cadence, messy provider replies, and invoice inbox chaos—not inability to find a vendor.

## Kill criteria (summary)

No touchpoint drop after fair shadow jobs; directory required; refuse human gates or demand unsupervised send; partner dual-approve permanently stalls (unfinished-job rate kills learning); near-zero pay-or-act; safety incidents make solo ops untenable.

## Synthetic falsifiers to watch in real tests

- Pain collapses for stable weekly cleaner once standing slot + instructions exist — value is exceptions + lumpy HVAC.  
- Partner never opens approval → unfinished jobs.  
- Phone-only providers force constant human relay.  
- Capacity to pay for time ≠ proven WTP for a chore PM vs free habits.
