# ICP: remote-care-or-multi-site-coordinator

**Name:** Remote / multi-site service coordinator (caregiver or owner-absent)  
**Round 1 rank:** 2 (strongest challenger)  
**Synthetic verdict:** weak_fit  
**promote_lean:** hold  
**Label:** SYNTHETIC ONLY — not PMF

## Who

Adult children, partners, or owners coordinating vendors for a residence they do not occupy full-time (e.g. aging-parent home, second property, or multi-site handoff). Hold decision rights and calendar constraints but depend on access codes, on-site contacts, and forwarded invoices. Composite buyer — not a property-manager SaaS vertical launch.

## Job to be done

Run a home-service job end-to-end from a remote seat: structured brief, gated outreach, quote normalize, money/time approval, confirmation, completion record, and a shareable status brief for household or on-site stakeholders.

## Pain

Async approvals without shared context, access/scheduling without presence, vendor status lost across inboxes, post-service admin (archive invoice, brief another party), and higher trust/safety surface when someone else is on site.

## Kill criteria (summary)

No coordinator touchpoint drop; directory required; access/trust blocks use even with gates; demand maps to multi-unit PM or eldercare SaaS; solo founder cannot recruit remote pipeline safely.
