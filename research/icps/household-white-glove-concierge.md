## Who

Metro-level composite: high-discretionary-income households already paying for premium home services, memberships, or human concierge help, who still drown in quote loops and calendar coordination for HVAC and cleaning. Household is the user and payer; not an operator. Public-safe composite only.

## Job to be done

Substitute or augment ad-hoc concierge habits with a gated Shape A chore PM: structured brief, dry-run drafts, quote normalize, approve-before-send, confirmed appointment, completion + next-due—measured by fewer touchpoints and rebook without re-research, not by vendor inventory.

## Pain

Time scarcity plus expectation of polished execution: multi-vendor quote comparison, strict money/time gates, calendar precision, and next-due discipline—willing to pay for relief in principle, but status-quo is often a human assistant, premium provider portal, or free AI chat rather than a durable job PM.
