## Who

Recent buyers or movers facing a short window of many first-time home-service jobs at once (HVAC check/tune, deep clean, locksmith, pest, minor handyman)—providers found externally; coordination is the firehose. Composite burst segment; not a private address.

## Job to be done

Run multiple house-service jobs in parallel with visible progress per job: brief each scope, draft gated outreach, paste/normalize quotes, prioritize spend and time, book windows, and close with a first-year rebook memory—without becoming a city directory.

## Pain

Concurrent open jobs, weak prior vendor memory, quote apples-to-oranges under deadline, calendar collisions with move logistics, and status chaos across email/forms—not a single standing weekly cleaner autopilot.
