**Single-DM** — Kill/demote if: fair shadow jobs fail to cut touchpoints vs one-inbox status quo; directory required; refuse gates / demand unsupervised send; near-zero pay-or-act after fair try; solo users power through with free tools and report no cognitive-load relief; standing cleaner + HVAC autopilot collapses continuous PM pain with no exception/lumpy residual; safety incidents make solo ops untenable.

**Move-in** — No touchpoint drop on multi-job bursts; directory/inventory required; refuse gates; post-burst rebook memory unused and no residual job-PM habit; access/empty-house incidents; systematically impossible mid-window recruiting.

**Landlord** — No owner-touchpoint drop; directory or full multi-unit PM stack required; access/trust incident; demand collapses to unstructured “just text”; near-zero pay-or-act; requirements drift into institutional PM (portals, ledgers, leasing); refuse gates or demand unsupervised send.

**Emergency** — Value requires live tech availability inventory/GMV; no touchpoint cut vs first-call thrash; refuse gates under urgency; phone-only path forces permanent human dispatcher; panic-dispatch only with no complete→next preventive; safety/diagnosis overclaim incidents; near-zero pay-or-act for coordination layer.
